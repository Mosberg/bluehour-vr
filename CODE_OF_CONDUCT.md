# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to VR, Python or open source.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, or sexualised language and imagery.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow
this code, and may ban people who repeatedly or seriously break it. Report problems privately through
GitHub's **Report content** feature or by contacting the maintainer through the profile at
<https://github.com/Mosberg>. All reports are handled confidentially.
